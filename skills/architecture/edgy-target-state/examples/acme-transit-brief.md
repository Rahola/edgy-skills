# Brief: Acme Transit ticketing renewal (fictional)

Acme Transit is a regional public-transport authority serving nineteen
municipalities, about 1.2 million trips a month. Strategy 2026–2030: mission
"Sustainable everyday mobility", vision "Most trusted way to move in the
region by 2030", focus areas *seamless travel chains*, *affordable and fair
fares*, *zero-emission fleet*; KPIs door-to-door time −15 %, one ticket for
95 % of trips, fare satisfaction ≥ 4.0/5, CO2 per passenger-km −40 %.

Current state: a legacy fare engine (contract ends 2027) sells card-based
season tickets; a mobile app and web shop sit on top; validators on board;
no travel account; integration is point-to-point. An eight-person platform
team defines and produces; suppliers build and operate; a procurement office
runs tenders; the board steers.

Open questions from the steering group: account-based travel (pay as you go),
open-loop bank-card payments, whether to re-tender the fare engine or extend,
and how to connect to the national timetable registry.
